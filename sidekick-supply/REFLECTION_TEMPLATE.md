# Engineering Challenge Reflection

Copy this into private notes for each task. Write it before requesting a review.

1. What did I initially think was happening?
2. What did I investigate first, and why?
3. Which evidence changed my understanding?
4. What is the root cause or key design decision?
5. Why does my solution or recommendation work?
6. What alternatives did I consider, and why did I not choose them?
7. What tests or observations demonstrate correctness?
8. Which edge case still worries me?
9. What concept do I need to explain again in 1, 3, 7, and 21 days?
10. What would I do differently next time?
