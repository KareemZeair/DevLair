# Personal Desk Motion: Retired Experiment

The Personal Desk cinemagraph has been discontinued at the developer's request.
Keep the original comic illustration, personalized plaque lettering, and clear
Workstation entry controls. No further video generation, Blender installation,
or animation plugins are planned.

Generic CSS shapes and a generated rotor overlay failed visual review and were
removed. The unused rotor image remains a historical experiment, not a live
asset. Runway was declined, and Blender was never installed.

The static scene continues to follow local time for ambient lighting. Day and
Night Shift continue to control application colors. Ordinary interface feedback
may use short transitions with reduced-motion support.

Separate illustrated day and night variants, desk sound effects, and additional
interactive decorations are optional future polish, not part of the current
checkpoint. Focus now returns to the review learning loop and small mentored
engineering changes.
